The work

Client and product work from Rev3 Labs, the design and development consultancy I co-founded in 2024. On most of these I was the only designer.

Zebre, medical imaging

A portal built on rules, not one-offs

Zebre finds the non-diagnostic data clogging hospital imaging archives, up to 75% of it. I designed the dashboard that shows IT teams where the savings are, and I'm now designing their customer portal with design-system rules so the rebuild doesn't inherit the old app's drift.

PublishRight, AI product

An AI proofreader, from brand to product

An AI proofreading platform for Christian publishers that learns each imprint's style guide. Proofreading that took weeks now takes about 20 minutes. I designed it from the ground up: the brand, the marketing site, and the product where editors review and accept the AI's changes.

Lee Company, field service

Letting customers book and pay on their own

About 80% of Lee's customers were dropping out of the old booking flow before they ever saw an open appointment. We redesigned self-scheduling around real-time availability, built Stripe payments and refunds into Dynamics 365, and I used Lee's own data to measure the adoption.
